      Accessibility

      Directions

        Twenty trips a day, three questions each time.

        1. Where am I?Find yourself on a map you've never seen, in a hall where every aisle looks the same.
        2. Where is it?Turn a vendor's name into a booth number, then a booth number into a spot on the map.
        3. What's the best way?Plan a route around walls and booths, and around stairs if you use a wheelchair.

        Once or twice a day, that's fine. Twenty to thirty times a conference day, it eats the working memory you came to spend on conversations. Lunzer answers all three in one step.

        Two ways to start.

        Type where you are

        Works in the demo above, on this year's expo floor. Enter a booth number, an exhibitor or a landmark like "coffee bar". Pick a destination and any accessibility needs. Lunzer finds the route and strips the map down to what's on it.

        • Typo-tolerant search over 237 booths and their exhibitors
        • Routes never cut through booths, seating or stages
        • Wheelchair routes that keep to wide aisles

        Let your phone find you

        Planned, not built yet. Open Lunzer and allow Bluetooth. Beacons in the hall place you on the map, so you only choose where to go. Press GO and follow live directions that update as you walk, until you arrive.

        • Bluetooth beacon positioning
        • Live, turn-by-turn navigation
        • Elasticsearch-backed search across the event's exhibitor list

        Live navigation isn't built yet.

        Stair-free is a route, not a footnote.

        This expo floor is level, so every route here is step-free. Turn on Wheelchair route and Lunzer keeps to wide aisles and stays out of tight gaps between seating and stands. Venues with stairs and lifts get a separate step-free course on the same map. Orienteering has a whole discipline, Trail-O, built so wheelchair users read the same map as everyone else. Lunzer takes the same stance: an accessible route is planned on the same map, with the same care.
